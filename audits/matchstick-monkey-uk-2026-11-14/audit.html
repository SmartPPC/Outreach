Priorities

What I would do first

  1. advertising

    Sonic toothbrush: push "baby toothbrush", "toddler toothbrush" and "electric toothbrush kids"

    The toothbrush (~£18,000 a month) ranks #21 for "baby toothbrush" (17,000 searches a month) and "toddler toothbrush" (15,600), and #30 for "electric toothbrush kids" (23,000), with no ads near the top on the first and third.

    What we saw
    • baby toothbrush: 16,988/mo, organic #21, no sponsored rank (Jungle Scout)
    • toddler toothbrush: 15,610/mo, organic #21, sponsored #16
    • electric toothbrush kids: 23,014/mo, organic #30, no sponsored rank
    • Family revenue ~£18,355/mo, 1,330 units, 4.3★ (Jungle Scout estimate)
  2. advertising

    Animal teether: push "baby teething toy" and "teething toys" from page one to the top

    The Ludo Lion teether ranks #6 for "baby teething toy" (12,900 a month) and #8 for "teething toys" (9,400) with no ads near the top. You already own "teething toys for baby" (#2 organic, #1 sponsored), so these are the next ones to win.

    What we saw
    • baby teething toy: 12,924/mo, organic #6, no sponsored rank
    • teething toys: 9,362/mo, organic #8; teething toy: 4,107/mo, #5
    • teething toys for baby: 48,828/mo, organic #2, sponsored #1
    • Animal teether family ~£18,599/mo, 4.8★ from 2,942 reviews (Jungle Scout estimate)
  3. catalogue

    Tidy-up: Teddy Tiger teether and Ludo Lion toothbrush are listed more than once

    The Teddy Tiger teether sits on two separate listings (2,952 reviews on one, 281 on the other), and the Ludo Lion toothbrush on three. Merging them puts the reviews and sales history in one place.

    What we saw
    • Teddy Tiger teether: B0CDX4RHKN (~£25,919/mo, 2,952 reviews) and B0CDX4JM5P (~£1,961/mo, 281 reviews), different parent listings
    • Ludo Lion sonic toothbrush: B0CZ9LR1XX (~£17,095/mo, 422 reviews), B0DB8Q7M35 and B0GMRGX4SQ (26 reviews)
  4. advertising

    Teethers and bath toys: cut ads on gifts, dummies and other brands

    The teethers show sponsored on searches for different products: "nuby" (6,000 a month), "giraffe gifts", "snuffle babe", "baby shower gift", MAM dummies and Bonjela gel, where they don't rank organically. Negatives free budget for the teething searches above.

    What we saw
    • Original teether B0FXBXDLPZ: nuby (6,014/mo), giraffe gifts (5,217), mam newborn dummies (2,329), ashton parsons teething gel (1,594)
    • Animal teether B07YXDNZSG: snuffle babe (5,336), baby shower gift (5,015), highchair toy (3,452), bonjela baby teething gel (1,916)
    • Toothbrush: kids toothbrushes (21,907/mo) sponsored with organic #140
