Priorities

What I would do first

  1. advertising

    Deep Roast Crunchy and Original Roast Smooth: cut ads on butter, hummus and Morrisons searches

    Both best-sellers show sponsored on searches for other products: "butter" (173,000 a month, organic #64), "morrisons" (46,000), "hummus" (17,000), "margarine" and "garlic butter". Negatives on these free budget for peanut butter searches.

    What we saw
    • butter: 173,146 searches/mo, sponsored #12, organic #64 (Jungle Scout)
    • morrisons: 45,974/mo, sponsored #22, not ranked organically
    • hummus: 17,448/mo, sponsored #16, not ranked
    • margarine: 7,568/mo, sponsored #17, not ranked
    • Also sponsored on spread, cashew butter, garlic butter, anchor butter, president butter
  2. catalogue

    Original Roast Smooth: a reseller holds the Buy Box at £11.99

    On the Original Roast Smooth jar (~£18,000 a month) the Buy Box is held by NBR TRAD£RS at £11.99, above the £9.00 list price, with 11 sellers, while the sister jars sell through Amazon at £5.95. Worth checking supply to Amazon and Brand Registry protection.

    What we saw
    • B0BCQCKK12: Buy Box owner NBR TRAD£RS, 11 sellers, price £11.99 vs list £9.00 (Keepa)
    • Deep Roast Crunchy B0BCQ91KNW: £5.95 via Amazon
    • Family revenue ~£18,380/mo (Jungle Scout estimate); Keepa sees ~£3,570, consistent with lost sales
  3. competitors

    Go after Pip & Nut shoppers on price

    Pip & Nut leads share of voice across your searches (16% vs ManiLife's 6%), and "pip and nut" (9,000 searches a month) has ManiLife at #22 with no ads near the top. At £5.95 against a £7.38 page-one median, the price makes the case for you.

    What we saw
    • Share of voice: Pip & Nut 15.8%, Morrisons 12.2%, Nutella 9.0%, ManiLife 5.9% (Jungle Scout)
    • pip and nut: 8,994 searches/mo, organic #22, no sponsored rank
    • Median page-one price: ManiLife £5.95 vs competitors £7.38
  4. content

    Deep Roast Crunchy: add a seventh gallery image

    The best-seller has 6 gallery images, one short of what shoppers swipe through on a phone. A comparison image (protein and ingredients against the big brands) would do the most work.

    What we saw
    • B0BCQ91KNW: 6 gallery images, mobile score 92 (Keepa)
    • Family revenue ~£28,536/mo, 4,796 units, 4.4★ from 638 reviews (Jungle Scout estimate)

Method

How this was put together

Built from public marketplace data (Keepa, Jungle Scout, DataForSEO, Amazon catalogue and bid recommendations) on 2026-11-14. We have not seen this brand's Seller Central or advertising account, so spend and budget figures are estimates.

Budget model: search volume × 12% of searches producing an ad click × target sponsored share of voice by tier × CPC benchmark, plus 15% for remarketing.
