## Email points

1. The Magic Body Oil is losing ground. Its Amazon sales rank has gone from around 5,400 in July to around 17,000 now, and it makes up most of your sales.
2. A lookalike is advertising on your brand name. A £9.99 "Magic Firming Body Oil" takes the ad slot when people search "besque", and you have no ads there yourself.
3. Ad spend looks aimed at the wrong searches. On "body oil" you're up against £4–£17 oils, while searches like "body oil for women" (4,300 a month, where you rank #16) have no ads behind them.
4. The 3.9-star rating is holding the price back. Page-one rivals average 4.6 stars, so lifting the rating would help every pound of ad spend work harder.
