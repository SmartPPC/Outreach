# Evidence: Besque (amazon.co.uk, 2026-10-20)

## 1. Original Magic Body Oil: act on the sales slide now

The body oil family (~£18k/mo, about 77% of revenue) has gone from a Beauty sales rank of ~5,400 in July to ~17,000 now, and is about 2x worse than a year ago, even though reviews keep growing. With one product carrying the brand, that slide is the main risk.

- B0DJH54BLF monthly sales rank (Keepa): Jul 2026 5,393; Aug 8,559; Sep 13,772; Oct 17,010 (Oct 2025: 8,243)
- Family ~198 units/mo at £50 (Jungle Scout, parent-level); Keepa shows 100+ bought last month on the single
- Family revenue ~£17,948 of ~£23,321 brand total (77%)
- Reviews 93 -> 1,011 over 13 months (Keepa)

## 2. Defend the BESQUE brand searches from a lookalike

A £9.99 'Magic Firming Body Oil' (3.6★) buys the ad slot on 'besque' searches, and BESQUE runs no ads on its own name or on 'besque magic body oil'. Brand-term ads are cheap and stop shoppers who are looking for you from buying a copy.

- 'besque': lookalike 'Magic Firming Body Oil, Compact Body Oil, Magic Luxury Body Oil…' sponsored at #4, £9.99, 3.6★ (DataForSEO, Amazon UK)
- Same product organic #6 on 'besque magic body oil'
- No BESQUE sponsored placement on either brand search

## 3. Move ad spend from generic 'body oil' to firming and crepey-skin searches

On 'body oil' (27k/mo) BESQUE sits at organic #27 and sponsored #38, against page-one rivals at ~£9.50 and 4.6★, so a £50 oil struggles to convert there. The premium fits shoppers looking for firming results: 'body oil for women' (4.3k/mo, organic #16, no ads), 'firming body oil' (#34) and rival premium firming oils like Nord Body (3.7k/mo).

- body oil: 27,417/mo, organic #27, sponsored #38 (Jungle Scout); page-one median £9.49 and 4.6★ vs BESQUE £50 and 3.9★
- body oil for women: 4,276/mo, organic #16, no sponsored rank
- firming body oil: 850/mo, organic #34; body oil for crepey skin: 1,105/mo, organic #31 (already sponsored #4)
- nord body multi peptide body oil: 3,701/mo, organic #65; cellu overnight firming oil: 3,319/mo, organic #62
- Sponsored on 51 terms in total, including 'body care' at ~£2.00 CPC

## 4. Lift the 3.9★ rating before scaling spend

With over 1,000 reviews at 3.9★ against a 4.6★ page-one norm, the rating is the brake on a £50 price point. The listing itself is strong (7 videos, A+, 5 detailed bullets), so the work is in the review drivers: what the low-star reviews say, inserts and expectation-setting in the copy.

- B0DJH54BLF: 3.9★ from 1,011 reviews (Keepa); variants 3.7–3.8★ (Jungle Scout)
- Page-one 'body oil' competitor median rating 4.6★
- Listing: 8 images, 7 videos, A+, 5 bullets averaging 218 characters

## 5. Give the new launches a proper start

The Unscented Magic Body Oil (18 reviews) and Luxury Shower Oil (28 reviews) make about £3k a month each. The shower oil already pays for a #15 ad slot on 'shower oil' (11.6k/mo) against established brands. A launch plan with Vine reviews and tighter ads would reduce the reliance on one product.

- Unscented family ~£2,950/mo, 4.1★ from 18 reviews
- Shower Oil family ~£2,700/mo, 4.0★ from 28 reviews
- shower oil: 11,566/mo; BESQUE sponsored #15, organic #83; ads above from Uriage, L'Occitane, Nuxe, SVR
