Bettervits

amazon.co.ukPrepared 2026-11-14

Bettervits advertises across most of its range, but its Marine Collagen (~£26k a month) has no ads at all, and spend is leaking onto searches like "c", "zinc supplements" and even a socket set.

Priorities

What I would do first

  1. advertising

    Marine Collagen: start advertising it on the collagen searches

    Marine Collagen (~£26,000 a month) shows no sponsored rankings at all, while it sits at #32 for "collagen supplements for women" (53,000 searches a month), #31 for "collagen tablets" and #47 for "marine collagen". It was also out of stock 13% of the last 90 days, so stock and ads need lining up together.

    What we saw
    • B09W82SY2B: 0 sponsored terms (Jungle Scout)
    • collagen supplements for women: 53,196/mo, organic #32; collagen tablets: 25,740/mo, #31; marine collagen: 27,799/mo, #47; collagen: 161,213/mo, #60
    • Out of stock 13% of the last 90 days (Keepa)
    • Family revenue ~£25,683/mo, 734 units, 4.1★ from 655 reviews (Jungle Scout estimate)
  2. advertising

    Creatine, Magnesium, Glucosamine and Omega 3: cut ads on unrelated searches

    The best-sellers show sponsored on searches they don't rank for: Glucosamine on "c" (120,000 a month) and "turmeric", Magnesium on "zinc supplements" (28,000) and "mag", Creatine on "creatine tablets", "pre" and "fitness", and Omega 3 on "3/8 impact socket set". This looks like auto or broad campaigns drifting; negatives free budget for Marine Collagen.

    What we saw
    • Glucosamine B09W7VZJ9Z: c (120,184/mo, sponsored #9), turmeric (20,543), mens vitamins (5,279) (Jungle Scout)
    • Magnesium B09W7TBGPH: zinc supplements (27,782, sponsored #13), mag (16,983), magnesium tablets (15,192)
    • Creatine B0CJ9TZ4V8: creatine tablets (21,877, sponsored #9, organic #102), pre (17,428), fitness (12,138), creatine capsules (9,030)
    • Omega 3 B0BJL68DMK: 3/8 impact socket set (1,651)
  3. content

    Every best-seller: add a brand video, a 7th image and shorter bullets

    Creatine, Magnesium, Glucosamine, Turmeric, Vitamin D3 and Biotics each have only influencer or customer videos, 5–6 gallery images and bullets that collapse on a phone. Creatine scores 60/100 on our mobile checks, with the title cut off before the size.

    What we saw
    • No brand-made video: Creatine, Magnesium, Glucosamine, Turmeric, Vitamin D3 + K2, Biotics (Keepa)
    • Gallery images: 5–6 on every best-seller
    • Bullets over 250 characters: Creatine (4), Magnesium (5), Glucosamine (5), Turmeric (5), Marine Collagen (5), Omega 3 (5)
    • Creatine B0CJ9TZ4V8: mobile score 60, title cut off before the size
  4. catalogue

    Metabolic+: build reviews behind a fast launch

    Metabolic+ is already selling an estimated ~£24,000 a month but has only 4 reviews. Enrolling it in Amazon Vine and review requests now protects conversion as ad spend scales.

    What we saw
    • B0HJ2W239T: 4 reviews, 4.5★ (Keepa)
    • Family revenue ~£24,213/mo, 692 units (Jungle Scout estimate); Keepa ~£17,495
    • Sponsored on 75 terms
  5. competitors

    Nutrition Geeks owns the shelf

    Nutrition Geeks takes 22% of the visibility on Bettervits' main searches against Bettervits' 3%. Their brand name (99,000 searches a month) already carries a Bettervits ad, so the comparison story (price per serving, strength) is worth putting in the images too.

    What we saw
    • Share of voice: Nutrition Geeks 21.9%, Solgar 6.1%, WeightWorld 5.6%, Bettervits 3.3% (Jungle Scout)
    • nutrition geeks: 99,091/mo, sponsored #64

Method

How this was put together

Built from public marketplace data (Keepa, Jungle Scout, DataForSEO, Amazon catalogue and bid recommendations) on 2026-11-14. We have not seen this brand's Seller Central or advertising account, so spend and budget figures are estimates.

Budget model: search volume × 12% of searches producing an ad click × target sponsored share of voice by tier × CPC benchmark, plus 15% for remarketing.
