Priorities

What I would do first

  1. advertising

    Baby bath, rinse cup and Ellie storage: cut ads on other bath products

    The bath range shows sponsored on searches for different products: foldable, collapsible and inflatable baths, bath chairs and cushions, Aveeno shampoo, and "bath water storage bag" (8,600 a month) on the Ellie toy scoop. Negatives free budget for the searches below.

    What we saw
    • Baby bath B084PP9W5F sponsored on: foldable baby bath (3,089/mo), baby bath chair (1,898), collapsible baby bath (1,638), baby bath cushion (1,406), inflatable baby bath (864) (Jungle Scout)
    • Rinse cup B0C9R3Z3YJ sponsored on: baby bath support (3,855), aveeno baby shampoo (1,138), aveeno baby wash (983)
    • Ellie B0C5RHGNTS sponsored on: bath water storage bag (8,595/mo)
    • Nappy bin B0GMQVL2SD sponsored on: snuggle ups (3,689/mo)
  2. content

    Baby bath: add a brand-made video and shorten the bullets

    The baby bath (~£56,000 a month, 4.7★ from 3,675 reviews) has 7 videos, all from influencers or customers, none made by Shnuggle, and 2 bullets over 250 characters that collapse on a phone. A short brand video showing the bum bump and backrest would lift conversion.

    What we saw
    • B084PP9W5F: 7 videos, all influencer or customer (Keepa)
    • 2 bullets over 250 characters; mobile score 75
    • Family revenue ~£56,121/mo, 2,283 units (Jungle Scout estimate)
  3. advertising

    Squishy changing mat: push "baby mat", "changing table" and "anti roll changing mat"

    The changing mat (~£58,000 a month) already ranks #5 for "changing mat", but "baby mat" (8,800 a month, #15), "anti roll changing mat" (#14) and "changing table" (7,900, #28) have no ads near the top.

    What we saw
    • baby mat: 8,756 searches/mo, organic #15, no sponsored rank
    • changing table: 7,933/mo, organic #28; baby changing table: 7,367/mo, #28
    • anti roll changing mat: 2,894/mo, organic #14
    • Family revenue ~£57,967/mo, 1,451 units (Jungle Scout estimate)
  4. competitors

    Brand search: show the rest of the range in the slots rivals are taking

    Shnuggle is #1 on its own name, but rival products (swaddle sacks, a nappy bin set, sleeping bags) take sponsored slots 4, 5, 11, 12 and 13 on "shnuggle", and a foldable bath takes slot 12 on "shnuggle baby bath". A brand headline ad showing the nappy bin, mat and bath keeps those shoppers in the range.

    What we saw
    • shnuggle: Cozyganoosh #4, Transitional Swaddle #5, 6-Way Omni Swaddle #11, Transitional Swaddle #12, Twist & Click nappy bin set #13 (DataForSEO)
    • shnuggle baby bath: SUPERBE BEBE foldable bath #12
    • shnuggle: 1,232 searches/mo, organic #1, sponsored #1

Method

How this was put together
