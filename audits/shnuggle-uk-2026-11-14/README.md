## Email points

1. Some of your ad spend is going on other bath products. Your bath range shows up as ads on foldable and inflatable baths, bath chairs, Aveeno shampoo and "bath water storage bag" (8,600 searches a month).
2. The baby bath has no video made by you. All 7 videos are from influencers or customers, and two of the bullet points are long enough to collapse on a phone.
3. The changing mat could reach more searches. It ranks #5 for "changing mat", but "baby mat" (#15), "anti roll changing mat" (#14) and "changing table" (#28) have no ads near the top.
4. Rivals are advertising on your name. Swaddle sacks, sleeping bags and another nappy bin take five of the first 13 spots when shoppers search "shnuggle".
