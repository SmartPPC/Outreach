# Evidence: W7 (amazon.com, 2026-10-21)

## 1. Move the 2026 calendar out of Home Décor

On amazon.com the 2026 calendar is listed under Home & Kitchen > Home Décor Products, while last year's edition sits in Seasonal Décor > Advent Calendars and the UK listing is in Make-up Sets. The wrong category hurts its rank and how Amazon matches it to calendar shoppers.

- B0GTRFKFV5 (US): Home & Kitchen > Home Décor Products, sales rank #6,663 (Keepa)
- B09RKF8DQK 2025 edition (US): Home & Kitchen > Seasonal Décor > Advent Calendars
- B0GTRFKFV5 (UK): Beauty > Make-up > Make-up Sets

## 2. Put ad support behind it

Only 1 of its top 30 searches has a W7 ad, and that one is 'stocking stuffers for teens'. It ranks #3 for 'beauty advent calendar' (66.8k/mo) and #6 for 'beauty advent calendar 2026' (99.4k/mo) with nothing behind it, right in peak season.

- Sponsored coverage: 1 of top 30 keywords (Jungle Scout)
- beauty advent calendar 2026: 99,424/mo, organic #6, no sponsored rank
- beauty advent calendar: 66,752/mo, organic #3, no sponsored rank
- amazon beauty advent calendar 2026: 73,498/mo, organic #7, no sponsored rank

## 3. Go after 'advent calendar 2026'

'Advent calendar 2026' gets 3.8 million searches a month and the calendar sits at #35. A top-of-search campaign, alongside the category fix, is the quickest way onto page one in the next eight weeks.

- advent calendar 2026: 3,848,176/mo, organic #35, no sponsored rank
- adult advent calendar 2026: 95,659/mo, organic #46
