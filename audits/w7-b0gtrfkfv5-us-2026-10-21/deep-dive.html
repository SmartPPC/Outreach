2. Category

  • It's listed in Home & Kitchen › Home Décor (rank #6,663).
  • The UK listing and the main rivals (e.l.f., Revolution, L'Oréal) are in Beauty › Makeup › Makeup Sets.

Not the main problem, but worth fixing. It already shows up well on beauty searches. Moving it to Makeup Sets would let it earn a Best Seller badge and rank against the right products. It's a quick Seller Support request.

  • Ads plus the price cut lifted sales without ads about 6× (about 7 a day to 41 a day).
  • Auto and competitor product targeting buy sales most cheaply.
  • Beauty searches convert well.

Not working

  • Every campaign pays more than $20 in ads per $27 calendar.
  • The general "advent calendar" campaign pays $43 per calendar.
  • Spend nearly tripled in two weeks, but returns fell to $0.95 of sales per $1.

6. Actions

  1. Cut the general "advent calendar" campaign from $1,000 to about $200 a day. Keep only terms that sell profitably. Add negatives: needoh, squishy, dumpling, chocolate, lego, for adults, amazon beauty advent calendar.
    Frees up about $800 a day. Today it loses money on every sale.
  2. Move that budget to the beauty searches where it already wins, at the top of the page. Use exact match on beauty advent calendar 2026, beauty advent calendar, makeup advent calendar 2026, make up advent calendar 2026, teen advent calendar 2026, with a +50–100% top-of-search boost.
    It ranks #3–10 organically here and takes up to 1 in 8 sales.
  3. Add competitor targeting for rival beauty calendars. Bid on searches for sephora advent calendar 2026, ulta advent calendar 2026, elf advent calendar 2026, essence advent calendar 2026, and expand the existing competitor product-page campaign to the best-selling rival calendars.
    These shoppers already want a beauty calendar, and W7's is a third of the price. Competitor product pages are already one of the cheapest sources of sales.
  4. Add Spanish searches such as calendario de adviento and calendario de adviento maquillaje.
    156k searches a month, page one organically, and they convert well.
  5. Set a limit on ad cost per calendar of about $10–12, depending on W7's margin, and lower bids on anything above it.
    It's $22–43 today.
  6. Tighten the $1,400/day Sponsored Brands video to beauty and brand searches only.
    It's the biggest budget.
  7. Ask Seller Support to move the calendar (and Holiday Haul) to Makeup Sets.
    Quick to do. It opens up the Best Seller badge.
