7Actions

  1. General "advent calendar" campaign: cut from $1,000 to ~$200 a day. Keep "advent calendar 2026" on exact match, low bid. Negatives: needoh, squishy, dumpling, chocolate, lego, for adults, amazon beauty advent calendar, amazon advent calendar.
    Saves ~$800 a day. Currently $43 per calendar.
  2. Green searches: exact match, +50–100% top-of-search. beauty advent calendar 2026, makeup advent calendar 2026, make up advent calendar 2026, luxury beauty advent calendar 2026, makeup advent calendar, advent calendar 2026 makeup/beauty, advent calendar 2026 teen girls, makeup advent calendar 2026 for teens, advent calendar 2026 women.
    Page one organically. Purchase share above click share.
  3. Competitors: expand product-page targeting to the top Sephora, Ulta, e.l.f., L'Oréal and Revolution calendars. Brand-name searches: small test only (Sephora and e.l.f. convert at about half the market rate).
    Rival calendars cost $60–95; W7's is $27. Product pages run at ~$23 per calendar today.
  4. Spanish: add calendario de adviento maquillaje and calendario de adviento.
    ~42k searches a week. Page one. Ratio 1.9–4.1×.
  5. Target ad cost: $10–12 per calendar, depending on margin.
    Currently $22–43.
  6. Sponsored Brands video ($1,400 a day): restrict to green and brand searches.
    Largest budget. Brand searches: 70–90% purchase share.
  7. Category: open a Seller Support case to move the calendar and Holiday Haul to Makeup Sets.
    Better traffic match. Best Seller badge eligibility.
