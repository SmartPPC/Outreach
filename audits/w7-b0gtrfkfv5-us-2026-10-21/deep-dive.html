What's driving sales, and where the ad money should go

21 October 2026

Each ad sale costs about $29 in clicks on a $27 product. Moving the money to the right searches is the biggest win, and fixing the category should help on top of that.

6Actions

  1. Cut the general "advent calendar" campaign from $1,000 to about $200 a day. Keep "advent calendar 2026" on exact match at a low bid only: it brings sales, but W7 converts at a third of the market rate there. Add negatives: needoh, squishy, dumpling, chocolate, lego, for adults, amazon beauty advent calendar, amazon advent calendar.
    Frees up about $800 a day. Today it loses money on every sale.
  2. Move that budget to the "Push" searches, at the top of the page. Use exact match on beauty advent calendar 2026, makeup advent calendar 2026, make up advent calendar 2026, luxury beauty advent calendar 2026, makeup advent calendar, advent calendar 2026 makeup/beauty, plus teen and women searches such as advent calendar 2026 teen girls, makeup advent calendar 2026 for teens, advent calendar 2026 women. Add a +50–100% top-of-search boost.
    Shoppers buy W7 1.1–2.8× more often than they click it here, and it already ranks #1–10 organically.
  3. Add competitor targeting for rival beauty calendars, led by product pages. Expand the competitor product-page campaign (already about $23 per calendar) to the best-selling Sephora, Ulta, e.l.f., L'Oréal and Revolution calendars. Add searches for those brands only as a small test: ulta advent calendar 2026 converts at market rate, while sephora and elf convert at about half.
    Reach shoppers already looking at a $60–95 calendar, with a $27 alternative.
  4. Add Spanish searches: calendario de adviento maquillaje and calendario de adviento.
    About 42k searches a week, page one organically, and 1.9–4.1× more purchases than clicks.
  5. Set a limit on ad cost per calendar of about $10–12, depending on W7's margin, and lower bids on anything above it.
    It's $22–43 today.
  6. Tighten the $1,400/day Sponsored Brands video to the "Push" and brand searches. Keep a small exact-match bid on w7 advent calendar.
    It's the biggest budget. Brand searches convert at 70–90% purchase share.
  7. Ask Seller Support to move the calendar (and the Holiday Haul) to Makeup Sets.
    Expect better matching to beauty shoppers, a likely lift in conversion on broad searches, and eligibility for the Best Seller badge.
