# Outreach email template

**Subject:** <specific observation, ≤ 8 words. E.g. "3 brands are bidding on 'Acme' searches">

Hi <first name>,

<Hook: 1 sentence tying to their job ad or a recent launch. E.g. "Saw you're hiring an Amazon
PPC lead to scale the new pet range, so I took a quick outside look at how it's showing up.">

A few things stood out:

- <Point 1: observation + number + implied fix, one line>
- <Point 2>
- <Point 3>
- <optional 4>
- <optional 5>

I've put the detail on one page here: <AUDIT_LINK>

Everything above is from public data. With access to your ad account and Amazon Marketing Cloud
we could confirm <the one thing AMC would answer that matters most to them>.

Worth a 20-minute call <this week / next week>?

<Signature>

---

## Rules
- Under 150 words in the body. Plain text, no images, no bold.
- Each bullet contains exactly one concrete number or named fact.
- Use their words: when they say "grow market share", say "market share", not "share of voice".
- Hedge estimates: "roughly", "we estimate", "looks like".
- Never: "I noticed your account…" (we haven't seen it), "guaranteed", client names, ACoS claims about
  them (we can't see their ACoS).
- Don't attack their current agency or staff. Frame gaps as opportunities.
